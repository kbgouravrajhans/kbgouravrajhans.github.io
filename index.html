RGB line balance study

The line's output depends on how machine speeds, conveyor accumulation and downstream capacity interact. I audited equipment and conveyors from the wet end through to the case packer, recorded machine speeds and accumulation, and used Pareto charts to sort the recurring losses.

Conveyor space
The dry end is meant to run with about 50% free conveyor, but the gap between labeller and case packer was too short to hold that.
Top sealer
Speed was capped by the bottom-taper carton-locking step.
Repeat losses
Crown changeovers, bottle falls and combiner jams.
